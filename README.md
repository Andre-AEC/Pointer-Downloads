# Pointer by AEC Simple

Pointer makes your cursor easier to follow during presentations and screen sharing. It adds an adjustable translucent circle around the pointer and brief ripples when you click.

## Download

The [public downloads folder](downloads/) contains the Windows installer and the full user guide together. For a one-click download, use the [latest release](https://github.com/Andre-AEC/Pointer-Downloads/releases/latest). The installer adds Pointer to your Start menu and starts its optional tray monitor at sign-in.

New to Pointer? Read the [user guide](downloads/USER_GUIDE.md) for setup, controls, screen sharing, troubleshooting, and removal.

Pointer requires the [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0). The installer is unsigned, so Windows may ask you to confirm that you want to run it.

## Features

- Adjustable circle size and transparency, with sliders and number fields
- Left and right click ripples
- Customizable shortcut to open or close Pointer
- Optional, experimental detection of Teams and Zoom screen sharing

The sharing detector may miss a share when presenter controls are hidden or after meeting app updates. When sharing a single app window, the meeting app may not capture Pointer's separate overlay.
